# Trigger sounds

The sounds a trigger can ring, bundled into `mudling-desktop`. Each is an
original tone made for MUDling with Python's standard `wave` and `math`
modules: decaying sine waves, with no samples from anywhere else. They're
dedicated to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

All are 16-bit mono WAV at 22,050 Hz.

| File | Length | Tones |
|---|---|---|
| `bell.wav` | 0.60 s | 880 Hz, with harmonics at 1,760 and 2,640 Hz |
| `chime.wav` | 0.65 s | 1,047 Hz, then 1,319 Hz, each with its octave |
| `alert.wav` | 0.52 s | Three short pulses at 740 Hz, with its octave |
| `low.wav` | 0.50 s | 196 Hz, with its octave |
| `pop.wav` | 0.09 s | 620 Hz, with 310 Hz under it |
